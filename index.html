GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, Answered

Do I need a medical card to shop?+

Yes — we're a licensed Oklahoma medical dispensary, so you'll need a valid OMMA patient license and a photo ID at every visit. If you don't have a card yet, visit omma.ok.gov to see how to apply.

How does the 10% first-order discount work?+

Every new patient saves 10% on their first order with us — just mention it when you check out in-store or pick up your order. And since tax is already included in our prices, the discount comes straight off the number you see.

Can I order ahead for pickup?+

Absolutely. Browse the menu here, tap through to any product on Weedmaps to place your order, and we'll have it ready. We also offer curbside pickup — just call us at (918) 306-0073 when you arrive.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept cash, and there's an ATM on-site for your convenience. Join our loyalty program while you're in and earn rewards on every visit.

Are your prices really tax-included?+

Yes — every price on our menu already includes tax. No surprises at the register, ever.
